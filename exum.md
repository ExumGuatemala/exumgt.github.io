# Exum

A business reference document for Exum — what the company is, what it sells, and who it's for. This is the source of truth to build the new exumgt.github.io site from.

- **Domain:** exumgt.com (current CNAME). A dedicated domain/email may replace the Gmail address below in the future.

- **Brand colors (confirmed from the logo source):**
  - Yellow: `#EDD011`
  - Gray: `#525252`

- **Naming convention:** all Exum products use an "i" prefix (iGroom, iWear, iCase) — a deliberate, Apple-style naming pattern. This is intentional and any future product would follow the same convention, though the founder isn't planning many more products beyond these three for now.

## Company

- **Legal status:** Exum is a registered company in Guatemala.
- **Mission:** build simple, easy-to-use software (SaaS) — specifically, software that solves one specific problem for one specific type of business well, rather than generic bloated tools.
- **Differentiator:** simplicity for a specific niche. Each product is scoped tightly to the workflows of one kind of business, so it stays easy to understand and use even when the underlying domain (e.g. textile manufacturing) is complex.

## Products

### iGroom — live, has its first client

- **What it is:** a SaaS for grooming businesses (pet groomers/salons).
- **What it does:** manages clients, pets, and appointments. Deliberately simple — solves one specific problem for one specific niche business.
- **Business model:** monthly/annual subscription.
- **Pricing structure:** planned as **3 tiers**. Only tier 1 is fully built; tiers 2 and 3 are planned to be completed during 2026. The founder wants to start selling tier 1 now rather than waiting for all tiers to be finished.
- **Traction:** 1 confirmed client (on tier 1).
- **Target market:** starting in Guatemala, expanding to Central America and then the rest of Latin America. iGroom is the one product the founder believes could eventually reach the US market, a few years out.

### iWear — live, in close collaboration with its first client

- **What it is:** an ERP for textile businesses.
- **What it does:** helps textile manufacturers track and manage clients, institutions, orders, products, materials, and inventory. Significantly more complex than iGroom (ERP-scale), but still built with the same "keep it simple and understandable" philosophy.
- **Business model:** monthly/annual subscription.
- **Traction:** one business is already using it and actively providing feedback/insights to improve it, given how complex textile-manufacturing workflows are. Effectively the first client, but in a tighter feedback-driven relationship rather than a standard sale.
- **Target market:** same expansion path as iGroom — Guatemala first, then Central America, then Latin America (no current plan for a US push for iWear).

### iCase — in planning

- **What it is:** a SaaS to help lawyers and law firms keep track of clients, documents, and cases.
- **Status:** in planning stage, not yet built.
- **Business model:** planned as monthly/annual subscription, same as the other two.
- **Approach:** expected to follow the same approach as iWear — working closely with an early/design-partner client to shape the product given the complexity of legal workflows, rather than building in isolation first.
